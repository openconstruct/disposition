Scaling

A single scheduler node dispatches around 5,000 jobs per minute with the
Postgres backend on a 4-vCPU machine. With Redis the figure is roughly 8,000
jobs per minute. Worker throughput depends on your jobs and scales
horizontally: add workers.

For availability, run two or three scheduler nodes. They elect a leader
through the backend; only the leader dispatches. Adding scheduler nodes does
not raise the dispatch ceiling.

Very large deployments usually split jobs across several independent
Larkspur clusters.
