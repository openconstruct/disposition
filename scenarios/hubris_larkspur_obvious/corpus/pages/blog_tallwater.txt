Moving our nightly jobs to Larkspur
Tallwater engineering blog, 12 June 2026

We ran about 1,400 scheduled jobs on a patchwork of cron hosts and a
homegrown queue. Last quarter we moved all of it to Larkspur.

What went well: cron expressions carried over unchanged, per-job timeouts
caught two jobs that had been silently hanging for months, and the dashboard
replaced a spreadsheet.

What was harder: we had to run the Postgres backend on its own instance,
because our shared database could not take the advisory-lock traffic during
leader changes.

We run three scheduler nodes and about forty workers. Dispatch has not been
the bottleneck.
