# 2. Demand

The waiting list is the most visible sign of demand, but it is not the only
one. Enquiries to the parks office have doubled since 2019 [2], and the Brayford Gardeners' Association reports that it's own
waiting list has grown at a similar rate [3].

Demand is not spread evenly. The two sites in the north of the town account
for nearly two thirds of the list, while the Riverside site has vacant plots
most years. This suggests that distance, not just supply, is part of the
problem. Many people on the list do not have a car, and the bus to Riverside
runs only once an hour.

It is worth noting that the list itself may overstate demand. The council
has not reviewed it since 2019, and some names are likely to belong to people
who have moved away or no longer want a plot. A review would give a clearer
picture, though it would take staff time the parks office does not currently
have.

"We don't even know how many of these people still want a plot," one council
officer told us.
