# 3. Land

The simplest answer to a shortage of plots is more land. The council owns
several parcels that could be brought into use, including the old depot on
Carter Street and the verge along Mill Road. Neither is ideal: the depot site
would need a soil survey, and the verge is narrow. But both are closer to the
north of the town than any existing site.

A second option is to work with private landowners. Two farms on the edge of
town have said informally that they would consider leasing land for
allotments [4]. This has worked elsewhere; in Castleford,
a similar scheme added 60 plots in three years [5].

The council should also protect the land it has. Three sites have been lost
since 2010, and none were replaced.
