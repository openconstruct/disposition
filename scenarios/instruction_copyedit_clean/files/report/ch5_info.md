# 5. Information

Finally, the council could do more to help people on the waiting list. At
present, applicants hear nothing between joining the list and being offered a
plot, which can be years. A yearly letter giving their position on the list
and an honest estimate of the wait would cost little and would be welcomed.

The council's website also lists sites that no longer exist, and gives a
phone number that has not been answered since 2021. Fixing this would take an
afternoon.

In summary, we recommend that the council reviews the list, brings the depot
and Mill Road sites into use, talks to local landowners, updates the tenancy
rules and writes to everyone on the list once a year.
