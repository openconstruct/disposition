# Travel and expenses policy

Owner: Finance. Effective 1 July 2026. Replaces all earlier versions.

## 1. Booking travel

Book all flights and hotels through the travel portal.

**Flights.** Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is
allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class is allowed only for
flights over 10 hours, and only with written approval from a VP.

**Hotels.** Up to $220 a night in the cities listed in Appendix A, $160 a
night elsewhere.

## 2. Meals

Meals are covered by a daily allowance, not by receipt:

- domestic trips: $75 a day
- international trips: $95 a day

Travel days count as full days.

## 3. Receipts

Keep an itemized receipt for every expense over $25.¹

## 4. Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the expenses tool within 30 days of the end of the trip.

- 31–60 days: still reimbursed; attach a short note from your manager.
- 61–90 days: reimbursed only with director approval.
- Over 90 days: not reimbursed.

## 5. What is not covered

Not covered: personal entertainment, minibar, upgrades you chose yourself,
traffic and parking fines, and alcohol.²

---

¹ Tips and parking meters are exempt: no receipt is needed for either,
whatever the amount.

² Exception: at a dinner with a client, alcohol is covered up to $40 per
person, including the client.
