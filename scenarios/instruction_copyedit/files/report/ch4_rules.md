# 4. Rules

The current tenancy rules date from 1998. They allow a plotholder to keep a
plot indefinitely, with no requirement to cultivate a minimum share of it.
In practise, a small number of plots are barely used for years at a time.

We recommend three changes. First, a cultivation standard, checked twice a
year. Second, a clear process for ending a tenancy when the standard is not
met, with warnings and time to improve. Third, the option of sharing a plot,
which would let more households take part without needing more land.

These changes would not be popular with everyone. "Some of us have had these
plots for thirty years," said one long-standing tenant. "You can't just take
them away." The council would need to consult carefully.
