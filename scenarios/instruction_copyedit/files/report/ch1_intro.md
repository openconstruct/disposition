# 1. Introduction

Allotments in Brayford have long been a quiet part of civic life, but the
waiting list has grown from 40 names in 2016 to more than 300 today. This
report sets out why, and what the council could do about it.

The reasons are not hard to find. Demand rose sharply during the pandemic, and
it has not fallen back. At the same time, three sites have been lost to
development and one to flooding, so their is less land to go round. The
council has tried offering smaller plots, but half-plots are unpopular with
the families who make up much of the list.

As one plotholder put it: "I waited six years for this plot. My grandson has
been on the list since he was born."

We argue that the council should act on three fronts: land, rules and
information.
