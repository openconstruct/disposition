# Roster rules

Shifts, every day of the week:

- early 06:00–14:00
- late  14:00–22:00
- night 22:00–06:00 (belongs to the day it starts on)

1. Every shift has exactly one person.
2. Nobody works two shifts on the same day.
3. At least 11 hours off between shifts. In practice: nobody who works a late
   or a night shift works the early shift the next day.
4. Everyone works 3 or 4 shifts a week.
5. Nobody works more than 2 night shifts a week.
6. Respect people.md.

The roster goes in roster/next_week.csv, one row per shift:

    day,shift,person
    Mon,early,Aiko
